# Business Rules
- Intern/team/task identifiers are unique. Default demo intern format is DIT-I###; no official DIT format was supplied.
- Task team must match intern team; assignment and due dates must be ordered; submission cannot precede assignment.
- Status: Not Started, In Progress, Submitted, Reviewed. Assessment: Pending, Passed, Need Revision, Fail (legacy Needs Revision values are also accepted). A Passed task must be Reviewed; Reviewed cannot remain Pending.
- Overdue = past due and not submitted/reviewed. Due today is separate. Upcoming window is configurable and defaults to 3 calendar days.
- Completion = Passed / all assigned tasks × 100. No cancellation policy supplied; no tasks are excluded.
- Attendance = Present / (Present + Absent) × 100; Leave and Holiday excluded.
- Admin scope is global, team leads are team-scoped, interns are self-scoped.
